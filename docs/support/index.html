Common questions

In Nari, open Settings → Partner and generate an invite code. Share the six-character code with your partner; they choose “I have a partner code” and enter it on their device. Codes are single-use and expire after one hour — if it lapses, simply generate a new one.

Yes. Your data is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud, protected by Apple's encryption, and you can lock the app with Face ID. Anything you share with a partner is encrypted with AES-256 before it leaves your device. Nari has no servers, no analytics, and no ads.

Only what you allow. You choose field by field — cycle dates, phase, mood, flow — and anything you don't grant never leaves your device. You can change permissions or revoke access entirely at any time.

Nari shows patterns, not predictions. It reflects what your body has been doing — cycle history, basal temperature shifts, moods, energy — so you can understand your own rhythm. It is not medical advice and not a contraceptive method.

If you enable it, Nari writes your menstrual flow to Apple Health so it lives alongside your other records. Nari never reads anything from Apple Health.

You can delete individual entries inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all local data, and your iCloud copy can be managed from your device's iCloud settings. Nari keeps no copy anywhere else.

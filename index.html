Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority.

Effective July 19, 2026 · Applies to the Nari iOS app

The short version

Nari is built so that your health data stays yours. Everything you record is stored on your device and synced only to your own private iCloud. We run no servers of our own, embed no analytics, and never sell or share your data with anyone. If you choose to share with a partner, that data is encrypted with AES-256 before it leaves your device.

What Nari stores

Nari records only what you choose to log: cycle and period dates, flow, symptoms, moods, energy levels, basal body temperature (BBT), and ovulation indicators. Nari does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity, and requests no such permissions.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Your data is stored locally and protected by iOS data protection, which encrypts it at rest while your device is locked. You can additionally lock Nari behind Face ID or your passcode.
  • In your private iCloud. Your cycle data syncs through Apple CloudKit to your personal, private iCloud database — accessible only with your Apple account. Apple encrypts this data in transit and at rest; we never see it.

On-device intelligence

All insights, pattern detection, and daily messages are generated entirely on your device. Nothing you log is sent to any external AI service or third-party server — Nari makes no network connections other than Apple iCloud.

Partner sharing, encrypted

Partner mode is entirely optional and under your control:

  • You decide exactly which fields your partner can see — anything you don't share never leaves your device.
  • Shared data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload. The encrypted payload is exchanged through Apple CloudKit; the contents are readable only by you and your paired partner.
  • Pairing uses a short-lived, single-use invite code that expires after one hour and is deleted the moment it is accepted.
  • You can revoke a partner's access at any time.

Apple Health

If you enable it, Nari can write your menstrual flow data to Apple Health so it appears alongside your other health records. Nari only writes — it never reads data from Apple Health. This connection is optional and controlled in iOS Settings.

Exports

You can export your data as a backup, PDF, or CSV at any time. Exported files are created on your device and shared only where you send them. Like any file you export, they are not encrypted — treat them as you would any personal document.

What we never do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data — with anyone, ever.
  • No accounts, no profiles, no data on our own servers. We don't have servers.

Your controls

You can delete any entry inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes your local data; data synced to your iCloud can be managed through your iCloud settings. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to retain once you delete it.

Medical disclaimer

Nari is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and its pattern observations and fertility estimates are not a contraceptive method and do not prevent pregnancy.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions? Reach us from the support page.
